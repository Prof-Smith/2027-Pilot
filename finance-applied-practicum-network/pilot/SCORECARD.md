# Founding-pilot scorecard

Use a 1 to 4 scale with narrative evidence.

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analysis | Major gaps | Partial | Sound | Decision-grade educational work |
| Model discipline | Opaque | Incomplete | Documented | Auditable and scenario-ready |
| Judgment | Unsupported | Basic | Evidence-based | Nuanced and risk-aware |
| Communication | Unclear | Uneven | Professional | Concise, responsive, persuasive |
| Partner value | Limited | Some | Useful | Strong value and return interest |
| Repeatability | Not viable | Major changes | Repeatable | Ready for disciplined expansion |

## Evidence to capture

- student submission and revision;
- practitioner feedback;
- faculty assessment;
- partner time burden;
- student reflection and career signal;
- recommendation: repeat, revise, expand, or stop.
