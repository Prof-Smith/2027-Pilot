WHY THIS, WHY NOW

Students need evidence of how they think, not only exposure to who they know.

The founding pilot converts an active external relationship into an assessed finance experience. Students will model a real decision, face practitioner challenge, revise the work, and leave with defensible evidence of professional capability.

01

02

Student teams create a model, scenarios, dashboard, and decision memorandum under faculty control.

FOUNDING-PILOT SCORECARD

Four questions determine what happens next.

Student capability

Analysis, modeling discipline, judgment, and written and oral defense.

Partner value

Usefulness, professionalism, time burden, and willingness to participate again.

Career signal

Portfolio evidence, clearer pathways, mentors, interviews, and internships.

Institutional learning

Course fit, faculty workload, safeguards, repeatability, and expansion decision.
