# Data, confidentiality, and AI standard

## Permitted data

- public information;
- synthetic datasets;
- historical data authorized for educational use;
- approved anonymized data with no re-identification risk.

## Prohibited without formal approval

- personal or client-identifiable information;
- cardholder or payment credentials;
- active transaction instructions;
- proprietary positions, pricing, contracts, forecasts, or security details;
- data restricted by law, contract, policy, or professional obligation.

## AI use

Students must disclose material AI use, validate outputs, retain source support, identify limitations, and remain responsible for all submitted work. Partner information must not be placed into unapproved AI systems.
