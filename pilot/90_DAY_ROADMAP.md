# 90-day launch architecture

## 1. Design
Confirm the partner, case, faculty lead, outcomes, safeguards, data boundaries, and measures.

## 2. Prepare
Train student teams, release the case, establish communication protocols, and complete the first analytical checkpoint.

## 3. Challenge
Hold a practitioner review focused on assumptions, missing evidence, model risk, and commercial realism.

## 4. Defend
Deliver the final decision panel, capture feedback, revise the work, and complete individual reflection.

## 5. Prove
Document student learning, partner value, career signal, lessons learned, and the recommendation to repeat, revise, expand, or stop.
