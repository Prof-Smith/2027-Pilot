## Decision rights

| Decision | Accountable role |
|---|---|
| Finance content and analytical standard | Finance lead |
| Course alignment and grading | Faculty of record |
| Partner communication protocol | Faculty lead and partnership liaison |
| Data classification and safeguards | Faculty lead with university review |
| External branding and logo use | Authorized university communications function |
| Pilot continuation or expansion | Academic and executive sponsors |

## Minimum controls

- signed scope or written project memo;
- approved data classification;
- student acknowledgement of confidentiality and AI rules;
- faculty review before external delivery;
- partner feedback captured through a common rubric;
- no publication of partner materials without written permission.
