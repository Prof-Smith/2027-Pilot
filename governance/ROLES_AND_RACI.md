# Roles and RACI

| Activity | Finance lead | Faculty of record | Partner liaison | Practitioner lead | Executive sponsor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Define finance standard | A/R | C | I | C | I |
| Align to course | C | A/R | I | I | I |
| Approve case scope | A | R | C | C | I |
| Manage students | C | A/R | I | I | I |
| Coordinate partner | C | C | A/R | C | I |
| Review external deliverables | A/R | R | I | C | I |
| Approve expansion | C | C | C | C | A |

A = accountable, R = responsible, C = consulted, I = informed.
