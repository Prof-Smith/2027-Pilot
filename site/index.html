RECOMMENDED FOUNDING PILOT

Embarc Collective: Venture Finance & Strategic Growth

One startup. One consequential finance decision. One accountable student-team experience. Three partner touchpoints across the semester.

Founder decision

Runway, unit economics, hiring, funding strategy, capital allocation, or milestone design.

Student evidence

Integrated model, scenario dashboard, funding matrix, decision memo, and practitioner defense.

Partner commitment

Case-design conversation, midpoint challenge, and final panel. Approximately three to five total hours.

  1. DesignConfirm case, scope, safeguards, and measures.
  2. PrepareTrain teams and complete the first checkpoint.
  3. ChallengeTest assumptions, evidence, and model risk.
  4. DefendPresent, receive feedback, and revise.
  5. ProveCapture learning, value, and repeatability.
